# Northwood Bids — Video Build Kit (real-world cut)
### Cinematic deals ad. Real products, real person, local pickup. No mascots, no gimmicks.

**Concept:** brand-name stuff for a fraction of retail — someone finds it, bids on their phone, wins, and picks up a haul at the local warehouse. Fast, warm, confident.
**Look:** cinematic realism, natural warm light, handheld, shallow depth of field, modern. NOT animated.
**Tagline:** *Real deals. Bid local. Northwood Bids.*
**Tool settings (not in the prompt):** aspect ratio **9:16**, max duration the model allows, sound **on**.

---

## Option 1 — One prompt (fast). Paste into Sora 2 or Veo 3.1.

> Written as filmed scenes, not app instructions. Keep on-screen text out of the AI (it garbles it) — add real captions in your editor.

```
Cinematic realistic ad, warm natural light, handheld with shallow depth of field, energetic and modern. A montage of a real person scoring deals through a phone auction app:

Beat 1 — Tight close-up of a hand holding a phone in a cozy living room; the thumb flicks through a grid of desirable products — a cordless power drill, an espresso machine, sneakers, a robot vacuum. The screen glow reflects in the person's interested eyes.

Beat 2 — Over-the-shoulder on the phone as they tap through a quick sign-up and a green checkmark lands; they give a small satisfied nod.

Beat 3 — Extreme close-up of the phone: a price counter ticks upward while a countdown timer in the corner drops into its final seconds; the person's thumb hovers, tense, then presses once, confident.

Beat 4 — The screen flashes a winning state and glows green; the person breaks into a grin and does a small fist-pump at their kitchen table.

Beat 5 — Daytime exterior of a clean, tidy warehouse; the person walks out and loads two or three boxes of goods into the back of an SUV, quick and easy, and shares a friendly nod with a staff member.

Beat 6 — Two friends on a couch laughing; one turns their phone to show the other the app; a small burst of confetti.

Beat 7 — Clean end on a warm, empty wall for a logo card.

Real people, realistic products with no visible brand logos, cozy homes and a bright warehouse, upbeat and aspirational, subtle modern music.
```

**Captions to overlay (bold, clean), in order:**
`Brand names — way below retail` · `Free to join` · `Set your max — never overpay` · `You won it` · `Pick up local, your schedule` · `Invite friends → up to $25 free` · `Real deals. Bid local. Northwood Bids.`

---

## Option 2 — Higgsfield Long Video (for a full ~60s). One entry per shot.

Feed these to the Long-Video / multi-shot generator; it sequences them. Same beats, one camera move each.

1. **Hook** — close-up hand flicking through desirable products on a phone in warm living-room light; screen reflects in the eyes. *Camera: slow push in.*
2. **Sign up** — over-the-shoulder of the phone; a quick sign-up completes with a green checkmark; a satisfied nod. *Camera: static, shallow focus.*
3. **Bid** — extreme close-up of the screen; price ticking up, countdown in the final seconds, thumb hovering then pressing. *Camera: slow zoom in.*
4. **Win** — screen glows green, person grins and fist-pumps at the kitchen table. *Camera: quick handheld.*
5. **Pickup** — bright warehouse exterior; person loads boxes into an SUV, easy, nods to staff. *Camera: slow orbit.*
6. **Refer** — two friends laughing on a couch, one shows the other the app, small confetti burst. *Camera: handheld.*
7. **End card** — clean warm wall for the logo. *Camera: locked.*

---

## Voiceover (Higgsfield Audio or ElevenLabs)
> Confident, plainspoken, a little bold. ~55–60s.

```
Brand-name stuff — for a fraction of retail. All up for grabs, right now.

It's free to join, and it takes about a minute.

See something you want? Set your max bid, and you only ever pay a little over the next person — so you never overpay.

Win it, and pick it up local, on your own schedule. Simple.

Bring your friends along, and stack up to twenty-five dollars in free bids.

Real deals. Bid local. Northwood Bids.
```

---

## Assembly (CapCut / Premiere)
- Drop the VO first; cut the beats to it (~8s each for a 60s spot).
- Add the bold captions, an upbeat modern track (duck under VO), and a clean end card: logo + **northwoodbids.com**.
- Export **1080×1920 · 9:16 · 30fps · H.264**.

**Note on AI + products/text:** the model will invent generic products and mangle any words on screens — that's fine, you're not trusting its UI. Show the *action* (thumb pressing, green win, loading the truck) and lay your real captions + logo on top in the edit.
