## The basics

**What is Northwood Bids?**
Northwood Bids is a local online auction business in Michigan. You bid on items
online; if you win, you pay, then pick up your items at one of our two pickup spots
(in Owosso and Gladwin).

**Do I need an account to bid?**
Yes. You have to create an account to place bids — there's no guest bidding.

**Do I need a card on file to bid?**
Yes. You add a payment card during setup before your first bid, and your profile
(name, phone, email) needs to be complete. You're only charged if you win.

**How old do I have to be?**
You must be 18 or older to bid.

## Bidding

**How does bidding work?**
Each item has its own end time and a live countdown. You place whole-dollar bids
(no cents). The highest bid when the item's clock runs out wins. If a starting bid
is $0, the first bid starts at $1.

**What are the bid increments?**
The minimum raise depends on the current price:
- Up to $11 → $1
- $12–$99 → $2
- $100–$499 → $5
- $500–$999 → $10
- $1,000–$4,999 → $25
- $5,000 and up → $50

**What is a Max Bid (proxy bidding)?**
You can set a private maximum, and the system automatically bids just enough to keep
you in front, up to your max. You only pay what it takes to win — not your full max.
Example: you set $85, but nobody bids past $25, so you win at $30, not $85. Your max
is private and never shown to others.

**Does the timer extend if someone bids at the last second?**
Yes. If a bid comes in during the last 2 minutes before an item closes, the item's
clock extends by 2 minutes so everyone gets a fair chance. It keeps extending as long
as people keep bidding in that window. (Applies to Max Bids too.)

**Can I cancel or retract a bid?**
No — bids are binding commitments. Once you place a bid, it can't be cancelled.

**What's a reserve price?**
Some items have a hidden minimum ("reserve"). If bidding doesn't reach it, the item
doesn't sell. The reserve amount isn't shown to bidders.

**What happens if I get outbid?**
You'll get a text/email letting you know so you can jump back in, with a link to the
item. (Alerts are batched over a short window, so if you re-take the lead quickly you
may not get one.)

**How do I know when an auction is live or ending?**
Registered bidders get a text when an auction goes live and a "last chance" text when
it's closing within the hour.

## Winning & payment

**How do I know if I won?**
When the auction closes you'll get a text summarizing what you won, and it'll show on
your dashboard ("My Bids").

**When and how am I charged?**
Automatically, right when the auction closes — your card on file is charged for
everything you won in that auction (one charge covering all your items).

**What's added to my winning bid?**
Your total is the winning bid **plus a buyer's premium and Michigan sales tax**. The
full "total if you win" is shown on each item's page *before* you bid, so there are no
surprises. **[CONFIRM: exact buyer's premium % and tax % if you want Woody to state
them — otherwise he'll just say they're shown before you bid.]**

**What if my card fails?**
No problem to fix — you'll get a text, and your dashboard shows a "Pay" button to
retry the charge (per item, or "Pay all" for everything at once). Your items are held
while you sort it out.

**Where's my receipt?**
You get a payment-received text, and the details are on your dashboard ("My Bids").

## Pickup

**How do I pick up what I won?**
Pickup is by appointment and you schedule it yourself. After your payment goes
through, go to your Pickup page (My Bids → Pickups), choose your location, and pick a
day and time.

**Where are the pickup spots?**
We have two — one in Owosso and one in Gladwin. The exact street address for your
pickup shows on your own Pickup page once you've won and paid (that's also where you
book your time). We don't post the street addresses publicly.

**How soon can I pick up?**
You can book any open slot within the next 28 days. Same-day pickups close at 8:00 AM
(Michigan time) so the team has time to pull your order — so if you book after 8 AM,
the soonest is usually the next open day. All pickup times are Michigan time.

**What does "staged" or "boxed and waiting" mean?**
It means the team has already boxed up your order and set it aside — when you arrive,
just look for the spot/box label shown on your Pickup page.

**Can I change or cancel my pickup time?**
Yes — you can reschedule or cancel on your Pickup page, and mark "I picked up my order"
when you've got it.

## Transfers between spots

**I won items at different locations — how does that work?**
You pick a preferred pickup spot, and everything you win is sent there automatically.
Items won at the other spot are transferred to your preferred one (usually about 5–6
days), and you'll get a text the moment they arrive and are ready to schedule.

**What do the transfer statuses mean?**
"Being gathered" = the team is pulling your items. "Loaded / in transit" = they're on
the way. "Arrived" = they're at your pickup spot and ready to book.

**Are some items non-transferable?**
Yes — a few items can't be moved and have to be picked up at the spot they're already
at. Those are marked, and they'll show under a "pick up at other locations" section on
your Pickup page.

**Can I switch my preferred pickup spot?**
Yes, anytime on your Pickup page. It re-routes your items to the new spot and clears
any scheduled time (anything already loaded on a truck keeps heading to where it was
going).

**What's a combo/pack lot?**
Some lots are a group of items sold together as one lot (shown with a photo collage).
Winning that lot means you get all the items in it.

## Bid Bucks (referrals)

**What is Bid Bucks?**
Our referral program. Share your personal invite link; when a friend who's new to
Northwood Bids signs up and wins their first item (and their payment goes through),
you earn $5 in Bid Bucks.

**How much can I earn and how do I use it?**
You can earn from up to 5 different friends — five $5 credits, $25 total. A credit
automatically applies to your *next* winning bill (on bills of $5 or more, one credit
per bill) — no code to enter. Bid Bucks have no cash value and can't be withdrawn.

## Account & login

**Where do I manage my account?**
Under your account/profile you can update your name, email, phone, and payment cards.
"My Bids" (your dashboard) shows what you've won and your pickup status; the Pickup
page handles scheduling.

**I forgot my password.**
Use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page. The reset link expires after 1 hour.

## Returns, refunds & problems

**Can I get a refund or return an item?**
All sales are final; refunds are handled case-by-case by the team. Woody can't process
returns or refunds — if you have an issue, he'll flag it and a team member will reach
out to help.

**Something's wrong with my item / I need a person.**
Woody will connect you — he passes it to the team and someone reaches out directly.
