# Northwood Bids — Business Setup Plan

**Decision:** Northwood Bids runs under Dakota's existing LLC (**A Eye Agency LLC**) as a
DBA. Dakota is the sole owner; Mom is paid as a contractor (1099), not an owner.

> Not legal or tax advice. One 30-minute CPA call is worth it to rubber-stamp the
> structure below (mainly: whose return the auction income lands on).

---

## Key principle
Whoever's tax ID is on the **Stripe account** is who the IRS treats as receiving
all sales income (Stripe issues a **1099-K** to that entity). So the Stripe account,
the bank account, and the DBA must all point at the **same owner — A Eye Agency LLC.**
Right now Stripe + bank are under Mom personally, so those need to move to the LLC.

A DBA is only a trade name — it does **not** create ownership. That's fine here
because there's one owner.

---

## Action checklist (in order)

### 1. File the DBA  ·  ~$10  ·  ~10 min
- Go to **michigan.gov/corpfileonline**, sign in, select **A Eye Agency LLC**.
- File form **CSCL/CD-541 (Certificate of Assumed Name)** — LLC fee **$10**.
- Assumed name: **Northwood Bids**. Submit + pay.
- Valid through Dec 31 of the 5th year, then renew. **Save the stamped PDF** (the bank needs it).

### 2. Open a business bank account
- Title it in the LLC name (often shown as "A Eye Agency LLC DBA Northwood Bids").
- Bring: DBA certificate (step 1), the LLC's **EIN**, Articles of Organization.
- **Biggest protection step** — running sales through a personal account ("commingling")
  is what lets someone pierce the LLC shield. Separate account = the shield holds.

### 3. Move Stripe onto the LLC
- Create a **new Stripe account under the LLC's EIN**, with the step-2 bank account for payouts.
  (Stripe won't let you change the legal entity on an existing account — it's a new one.)
- Note: just changing the payout *bank* is NOT enough — if the account is still under Mom's
  SSN, the 1099-K still goes to her. It must be the LLC's EIN.
- Do it **between auctions** so nothing is interrupted.
- **Claude reconnects the new account to the site** (Stripe Connect) so payments keep flowing.

### 4. General liability insurance
- Quote from any small-business insurer (or a Business Owner's Policy).
- Tell them: online auction / liquidation, customers pick up on-site at **2 locations
  (Owosso + Gladwin)**. The LLC protects personal assets; insurance protects the business
  from the claim itself.

### 5. Pay Mom
- Pay her from the business account. If $600+/year, issue a **1099-NEC** at year-end.
- It's a deductible business expense for the LLC; she handles her own taxes on it.
- The Reports screen's per-location numbers (Gladwin) are a fair basis for her pay.

### 6. Google Business Profile — LAST (not urgent)
- Do only after the name is locked. Verification (postcard) can take 1–2 weeks, so it just
  runs in the background.
- Create **two listings** — Owosso and Gladwin — verify each, category **"Auction House"**,
  add website, pickup hours, photos. Keep name/address/phone identical everywhere.

- **Claude:** reconnects the new LLC Stripe account to the site the moment it exists (step 3).
