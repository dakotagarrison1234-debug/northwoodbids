# Northwood Bids — "Not Your Normal Auction" (Facebook ad)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical (Reels, Stories, and feed) · ~55 seconds · burned-in captions (most people watch muted)
**Tone:** dry, self-aware, fast cutaways, with mid-Michigan humor (deer camp, up north, January trucks, nightcrawlers). Clean, family-safe. **Voice:** natural, warm Midwestern guy performed in-scene. No robotic text-to-speech. The host is an original character, not a celebrity look-alike or sound-alike.
**Host:** "The Northwood Guy." Early 40s, flannel shirt under a slightly-too-nice blazer, dry delivery, raises one eyebrow a lot. He stands in a warehouse aisle stacked with sealed name-brand boxes.

---

## Why these points (from the research)

Nearby Michigan liquidation auctions charge 15–20% premiums with surprise fees. Their listings are as-is, pickup windows are tight (miss them and you forfeit), and nobody moves items between locations. The #1 complaint across BidFTA, Nellis, and similar sites is items that are broken, missing parts, or not as described. Northwood answers each one: 99% brand new, the box gets opened and described, free Owosso↔Gladwin transfers, charged only if you win, the "Total if you win" price is shown upfront, and late bids add time. Nobody nearby has a referral reward (Bid Bucks) or anything like Bid Critters.

## Fact check (every claim matches the live site)

- **99% brand new, name-brand, still in the box:** home page "99% brand new"
- **We open the box and tell you what's in it:** home page "Straight conditions"
- **Every lot starts at $2:** home page and Help
- **Charged only if you win:** Help, "When is my card charged?"
- **Free transfers between Owosso and Gladwin:** Help, "What if a lot is stored at the other location?" (transfers take about 5–6 days)
- **Last-2-minute bid adds 2 minutes:** Help, "Why does the clock sometimes add time at the end?"
- **Bid Critters:** Account page avatar picker
- **$5 Bid Bucks per friend:** Help. The friend has to sign up, win, and pay. Capped at 5 friends/$25. The script says "earn," not "free money."
- **Sign-up free, about a minute:** Help, "Signing up takes about a minute"
- **Not claimed on purpose:** no buyer's premium %, no shipping (there isn't any), and no "no fees" line (a premium and tax apply).
